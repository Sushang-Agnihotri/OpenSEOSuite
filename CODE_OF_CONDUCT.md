# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience level, background or identity. Participants are
expected to be respectful, constructive and patient in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's security/contact channel. Reports are handled confidentially.
